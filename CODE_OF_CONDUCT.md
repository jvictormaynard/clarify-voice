# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ClarifyVoice should be a welcoming place to learn, build, review, and disagree
constructively. Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate with respect and patience;
- give actionable feedback about the work, not the person;
- welcome different levels of experience and different backgrounds;
- accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes;
- protect private information shared during debugging.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and
other spaces where someone is representing ClarifyVoice.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jvictormaynard). Include
links or screenshots when safe to do so. Reports will be handled as privately
as practical and reviewed for context.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, issue a warning, limit
participation, or permanently ban someone depending on severity and pattern.
Good-faith reports and appeals will not be retaliated against.
